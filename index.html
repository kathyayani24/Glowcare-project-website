Frequently Asked Questions

A few simple answers about being part of GlowCare.

How does GlowCare use my data?

We use the details you provide to manage your subscription and send the updates you request. Please read the privacy information on our Join GlowCare and Contact pages.

How can I unsubscribe?

You can unsubscribe using the link in a marketing email or contact us at sales@glowcare.com to withdraw your consent.

Is GlowCare's advice medical advice?

No. Our content is general skincare information and is not a diagnosis or medical advice. Contact a qualified healthcare professional about persistent or concerning skin issues.

How often will GlowCare email me?

We aim to send occasional, useful updates rather than frequent messages. The frequency may vary with launches and offers.
