Your simple Skin Guide

Everyone's skin is different. These gentle tips can help you think about your routine and what you may want to explore.

Five common skin types

Your skin can also change with seasons, environment and other factors.

Oily

Try a gentle cleanser and lightweight products that feel comfortable on your skin.

Dry

Choose a moisturizer that helps your skin feel comfortable, and avoid overly harsh cleansing.

Combination

Keep your routine balanced and adjust products to the areas that need different care.

Normal

Keep things simple and consistent with gentle cleansing, moisture and daytime sun protection.

Sensitive

Introduce new products slowly and consider fragrance-free options with simple ingredient lists.
